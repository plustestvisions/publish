Hi dear reader! My name is Paul van Haaster and I live in a town called Nijmegen in the Netherlands.  
Since 1996 I am working in the software development domain in several roles. Since 2006 as a software tester and since 2022 as scrum master too.  

But my heart is with software testing as it is fun and it is great to making my colleagues aware of possible risks in the product and helping them to make decisions on that information. I am currently working for [Planon](https://planonsoftware.com/) as a senior test engineer, and scrum master in one of the Planon framework teams.

Software testing for me means:</li><ul><li>Deliver information on the status of software product to anyone important</li><li>Critical thinking on all aspects of software development</li><li>Enable testing by taking ownership, coaching, support and teach</li><li>Explore and Experiment the product in broadest sense</li><li>Help the team to improve and reduce waste</li><li>Being aware of Quality in the broadest sense</li></ul>  

Keywords: open, direct, result driven, eager to learn new stuff, curious.   

Next to software testing I love to bike on my racing and gravel bikes, reading, good food and drinks and visit live concerts and listen to music.
