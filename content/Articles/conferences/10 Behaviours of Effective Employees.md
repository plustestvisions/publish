# Introduction
Every manager dreams of having the best and most effective employees they can find. A dream team of people capable of achieving so much with so little strain on management.
They dream of the perfect culture where people work well together, have fun, delivery amazing business results and respect each other.
It’s perfection – but hard to achieve.
But it’s easier when you focus on behavior's.
# Learnings
- what we do
	- the actions we perform
- What we say (the words we use)
	- These affects business results, retention and other people's behaviors
	- Just do it
- How we say it
	- speed, tone, inflection, confidence, pitch
	- it's not what you said, it's how you say it
- Body language
	- Deviating from baseline
	- congruent with message
- Work output
	- quality, quantity, timeliness, communication
	- relationships

10 behaviors
1. be visibly passionate
	1. fun, show passion positively
2. be open minded
	1. admit that you have no clue what the right direction is, adapt to the winds of change
3. draw a frame around yourself
	1. be clear about your role(s) description(s), learn between the roles
4. become company smart
	1. wider awareness results in less surprises
5. learn who your customers are
6. improve the process
	1. example of the customer support process: start on 14 days and improve to 1,5 days
7. do what you say you will
8. communicate
	1. communication has: an audience, a purpose, a context
9. add skills
	1. have multiple skills, no need to be excellent in all of them
10. be brave. 
	1. opposite = conformity
