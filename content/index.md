Welcome to plustestvisions where I will share my thoughts on software testing.  
Who am I? I am Paul van Haaster, software tester since 2006. For more information see [[about/about]]
Last year I got the idea to start some kind of a blog on my journey as a software tester. As I am keeping a journal for every working day using obsidian (since 2023 thanx to [Nicole van der Hoeven's](https://nicolevanderhoeven.com/) excellent talk at Eurostar testing conference in Antwerp) I want to use obsidian to create, manage and publish content from.  
So during my december holiday this year I explored using quartz to publish articles to GitHub using Quartz. So here you are looking at my blog, being in the experimental phase.
